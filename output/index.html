Payment Gateway

Integrate a secure payment gateway to facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers, with options for credit card, PayPal, or other payment methods.

User Profiles

Create user profiles to showcase your listings, purchase history, and ratings, with options for profile customization and privacy settings.

Messaging System

Develop a messaging system for buyers and sellers to communicate with each other, with features for responding to inquiries, negotiating prices, and arranging meetups.
