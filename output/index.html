Features

Listings & Pricing Engine

Create, edit, and expire textbook listings; AI‑driven price‑suggestion based on ISBN, condition, and recent market data; automatic expiration after 30 days with optional renewal. Non‑functional: supports 5 k concurrent listing edits, data stored encrypted at rest.

Search & Discovery

Full‑text search by ISBN, title, author, and filters (campus, price, condition). Results delivered <200 ms latency; cached for peak enrollment spikes. Scalable to 20 k simultaneous queries.

Messaging & Notifications

In‑app real‑time chat, push‑notifications, email & SMS alerts for new offers, messages, and transaction status changes. GDPR‑compliant opt‑out handling.

Payments & Escrow

Stripe integration for credit/debit cards and ACH; platform holds buyer payment in escrow, releases to seller after confirmed receipt. Commission 8 % + $0.30 per transaction; dispute workflow with 48‑hour resolution window.

Admin & Analytics Dashboard

Moderator tools to flag/invalidate listings, manage disputes, and monitor fraud patterns. KPI widgets: listings/week, conversion rate, avg. time‑to‑sale, churn. Exportable CSV reports. SLA: 99.9 % uptime.
