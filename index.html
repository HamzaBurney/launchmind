Buy & Sell Second-hand Textbooks Easily

A user-friendly platform empowering students to find affordable textbooks, create detailed listings, and complete secure transactions with confidence.

Platform Features

  • User Profiles

    Allow students to create and manage profiles with contact info, transaction history, and ratings.

  • Listing Creation and Management

    Enable users to create textbook listings with details such as title, author, edition, condition, price, photos, and course relevance, and edit or delete listings.

  • Advanced Search and Filters

    Provide powerful search capabilities with filters for subject, course code, author, price range, condition, and seller rating.

  • Secure Transaction Handling

    Integrate payment gateway for secure payments, support escrow functionality to protect buyers and sellers, and provide order tracking.

  • Messaging and Notification System

    Enable direct messaging between buyers and sellers, plus notifications for listing updates, new inquiries, and transaction progress.
