Key Features

Student Verification & Registration

Secure sign‑up using university email or single sign‑on (SSO); automatic validation against registrar records to confirm active student status.

Listing Creation Wizard

Step‑by‑step UI for sellers to enter title, author, edition, ISBN, condition rating, price, and upload up to 5 photos; supports bulk import via CSV for power sellers.

Search, Filter & Course Mapping

Full‑text search with filters for ISBN, price range, condition, and automatically suggested listings based on the user’s enrolled courses.

In‑App Messaging & Escrow Payment

Secure messaging between buyer and seller; integrated payment gateway holds funds in escrow, releases to seller after buyer confirms receipt, with a 10% platform commission.

Rating, Review & Admin Moderation Dashboard

Post‑transaction star rating and written review; admin UI for dispute resolution, content removal, and campus‑specific policy enforcement.
